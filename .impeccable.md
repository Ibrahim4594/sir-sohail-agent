## Design Context

### Users
- **Prof. Sohail** (Eastern Michigan University) — project owner. Uses Sir Sohail Agent to demo the product to funders and to work through the corpus himself. Needs the landing page to look "real-world app, not a demo, not AI-generated."
- **EMU students** — signed-in users. Ask questions of the PDF corpus and read cited answers. Landing page is their entry point on first visit.
- **Funders / visitors** — unauthenticated. Land on the homepage with zero context for the product; the landing has to explain what Sir Sohail Agent is, build trust, and drive them to sign in within one viewport of reading.

Context of use: desktop-first (academic workflow, funder demos). Dark or light viewing varies — support both, don't glow.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **cited · measured · academic**.

- "Cited, not guessed" is the actual tagline — the brand is fundamentally about honesty and provenance.
- The product is named after Prof. Sohail himself — it's his curated library of papers, and the tool carries his name. Scholarly product that treats citations as first-class.
- Voice: confident, low-volume, factual. No marketing exclamation points. No "Supercharge your research with AI." No emoji.
- Emotional goal: the reader trusts the product before they've used it. They should feel they're looking at a research tool built by people who read papers, not by a growth team.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: light (academic default — funders and professors read on white backgrounds). Dark mode supported but light is the canonical look.
- **Palette**: strict monochrome. `--foreground` (near-black, slightly tinted), `--background` (near-white, slightly tinted), `--muted-foreground`, `--rule` (faint neutral line), `--border` (slightly stronger). No accent color. No gradient. No colored text.
- **Typography**: currently Instrument Sans/Serif. Per impeccable guidance those are on the reflex-fonts-to-reject list — we should move off them to something more distinctive. Target pairing: a well-drawn working-grade sans for body/UI + an editorial serif for display, both with real character (e.g., Söhne-alike or Editorial-alike or actual Pangram Pangram / Klim / Velvetyne face). Absolutely no Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Geist, Satoshi.
- **Motion**: restrained. A single staggered entrance on landing load. No scroll-triggered parallax. No micro-hovers everywhere. Reduced-motion honoured.
- **References (DO)**: Anthropic.com, Linear.app, Vercel.com, ChatGPT.com empty state. All three land a thesis in one sentence, one clean hero image or typographic statement, and move on.
- **Anti-references (DO NOT)**: purple gradient heroes, hero metric grids, icon+heading+paragraph feature cards, "Powered by AI" badges, glowing CTAs, animated gradients on headlines, stacked product screenshots.

### Design Principles
1. **Type carries the page.** No decorative illustration, no stock photography, no 3D renders. If the type doesn't hold the page, the page fails.
2. **Monochrome only.** Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, and space — never from colour. The only "colour" on the page is the user's future Google OAuth avatar.
3. **One thesis per viewport.** The first screen says what Sir Sohail Agent is in one sentence. No feature lists above the fold.
4. **Citations as proof.** The landing should show, not tell. A visible citation or excerpt beats a marketing claim every time.
5. **Nothing animates without reason.** Motion is reserved for confirming a state change (entrance, navigation, focus). No ornament loops.
